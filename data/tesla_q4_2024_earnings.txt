Tesla Inc. Q4 2024 Earnings Report

Financial Highlights
Tesla reported Q4 2024 revenue of $25.9 billion, up 8.2% year-over-year from $23.9 billion. Full-year 2024 revenue reached $98.7 billion, compared to $96.8 billion in 2023. Total vehicle deliveries in Q4 were 484,507 units, with full-year deliveries of 1.84 million vehicles.

Automotive Segment
Automotive revenue was $21.6 billion for Q4, with automotive gross margin (excluding regulatory credits) at 18.2%. This represents an improvement from 17.6% in Q3, driven by manufacturing cost reductions and improved pricing dynamics. Regulatory credit revenue was $890 million in Q4.

The Model Y remained the best-selling vehicle globally, with the refreshed Model Y contributing to improved average selling prices. The Cybertruck achieved positive gross margin for the first time in Q4, producing approximately 42,000 units during the quarter.

Energy and Storage Segment
Energy generation and storage revenue reached $3.1 billion in Q4, a 67% increase year-over-year. The Megapack and Powerwall product lines drove strong demand. Gross margin for the energy segment expanded to 30.5%, making it Tesla's highest-margin business segment. Total energy storage deployments were 11.2 GWh for Q4.

Operating Expenses
Total operating expenses were $2.8 billion in Q4. Research and development spending was $1.2 billion, focused on next-generation vehicle platforms, Full Self-Driving technology, and the Optimus humanoid robot program. SGA expenses were $1.6 billion.

Profitability Metrics
Operating income was $2.8 billion with an operating margin of 10.8%. Net income attributable to common stockholders was $2.5 billion, or $0.73 per diluted share. GAAP operating margin improved sequentially from 9.2% in Q3 to 10.8% in Q4.

Cash Position
Tesla ended Q4 with $29.1 billion in cash and investments. Operating cash flow was $4.4 billion, and free cash flow was $2.1 billion. Capital expenditures totaled $2.3 billion, primarily for Gigafactory expansions and new production equipment.

Comparison with Ford Motor Company
For comparative context, Ford Motor Company reported Q4 2024 revenue of $43.2 billion with an automotive gross margin of 6.8% and an operating margin of 3.1%. Ford's F-Series maintained its position as the best-selling truck line in North America with 198,000 units delivered in Q4. Ford's total vehicle deliveries were 1.02 million units for Q4, significantly higher volume but at lower margins compared to Tesla.
